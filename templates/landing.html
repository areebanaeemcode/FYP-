Why Pay-Together

Everything You Need for Peaceful Group Travel

No messy spreadsheets, no confusing WhatsApp chats, and zero forgotten expenses.

Minimal-Transfer Algorithm

Instead of 10 people sending 20 separate payments to each other, our graph greedy engine simplifies all debts into the absolute minimum number of cash/bank transfers.

Works In No-Signal Valleys

Traveling through Babusar Pass, Deosai, or Khunjerab with zero cell service? Add expenses offline seamlessly. Everything auto-syncs as soon as 4G returns.

Equal or Custom Splits

Split equally across all members or designate custom shares for individuals (e.g. someone didn't eat dinner, or paid extra for a private hotel room).

1-Click Join Codes

No tedious invites or email confirmations needed. Share a clean 6-character token (e.g. HNZ420) in your WhatsApp group to add friends instantly.

Budget & Category Analytics

Track your spending against the allocated tour budget in real time. Breakdown totals by Transport (Petrol/Jeeps), Accommodation, Food, and Activities.

Receipt & Slip Attachments

Snap a photo of the petrol pump slip, hotel invoice, or restaurant bill and attach it directly to the expense. Eliminates doubt and keeps full transparency.
